# Official Legal Protections & Fraud Prevention Policy

This document outlines the legal framework governing Cursory AIP. we maintain a zero-tolerance policy fraudulent
activities, brand impersonation, and identity theft. all violations are reported to the indian cyber crime cell under the infromation
technology Act and Bharatiya Nyaya sanhita (BNS).

## 1.  Protection against brand impersonation
Any individual or entity attempting to use the name "Cursory AIP" or its logo to deceive users through fake emails, 
SMS, or message will face strict action.

| Violation Type | Legal Section (India) | Maximum Punishment | 
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cheating theft | IT Act section 66C & BNS section 319 | up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹1,00,000 file |
| Identity theft | IT Act section 66C | up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹1,00,000 file |
| Forgery of Documents | BNS section 336 / 340  | up to 7 years imprisonment + fine |

## 2. User Fraud & Systems Manipulation
User attempting to cheat the platform via bot traffic, fake views, or deceptive link submissions are subject to the following penalties.

| Action | platform penalty | legal action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bot traffic/fake views | permanent ban & wallet recovery | BNS section 318 |
| Hacking/system manipulation | account termination | IT Act section 66 (cyber crime) |

** IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE:** Cursory AIP will NEVER ask for your social media password, bank OTP, or personal login credentials. we only require public profile links for verification. any loss incurred by sharing password with third parties is the sole responsibility of the user.

## 3. Data protection & Privacy
* **Bio-code verification:** Our verification process is automated and safe. we do not access your private messages or account settings.
*  **Data erasure:** Upon account deletion, all user data (social links, wallet history, personal info) is permanently wiped from our secure as per Indian data protection standards.

## 4. Financial liability & disclaimer
Once a withdrawal is processed to the bank/UPI details provided by the user, the transaction is final and irreversible. Cursory AIP is not responsible for funds lost due to incorrect details provided by the user (referencing the Indian Contract Act)
